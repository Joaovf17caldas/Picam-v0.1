# Changelog

All notable changes to PICAM are documented in this file.
Versioning follows the pattern `MAJOR.MINOR`. Versions below 1.0 are public drafts under doctoral validation.

## [v0.1] — 2026-11 — Public draft, under doctoral validation

### Added
- `README.md`: definition of PICAM, the eight principles, the seven phases (F0–F6) and gates (G0–G6), the three-layer architecture, license and status.
- `manual/PICAM-Manual-Overview.md`: overview of phases F0–F6 (objectives, activities, deliverables, PAM controls, exit criteria), roles, cross-cutting PAM layer with integration matrix, PICAM-PMM levels L0–L4 and template catalog.
- `checklists/`: gate checklists G0 (Initiation and governance) and G1 (Assessment and privilege inventory).
- `templates/`: T-05 Privileged Accounts and Credentials Inventory (Markdown + CSV); PICAM-PMM maturity questionnaire (21 draft indicators).
- `crosswalk/`: draft mapping to NIST CSF 2.0 and NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5.
- `LICENSE` (CC BY 4.0) and `CITATION.cff`.

### Known limitations
- Not validated. Content validation (expert panel) and field evaluation (multiple-case study) are pending.
- Questionnaire wording and scoring are provisional.
- Crosswalk identifiers to be verified against official NIST references.
