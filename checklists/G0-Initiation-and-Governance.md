## Instructions

1. Check each item only if the **evidence** listed exists and can be shown.
2. Every item marked **[M] mandatory** must be met to record the decision "proceed".
3. Items marked **[R] recommended** may be left open with a justification.
4. Record the gate decision at the end.

## A. Sponsorship and roles

- [ ] **[M]** Executive sponsor formally appointed — *Evidence: signed project charter (T-01) or meeting minutes.*
- [ ] **[M]** Adoption lead appointed — *Evidence: T-01.*
- [ ] **[M]** Privileged access owner designated **by name** — *Evidence: T-01 or Privileged Access Policy (T-04).*
- [ ] **[M]** RACI matrix defined, including the IT service provider when applicable — *Evidence: RACI document.*
- [ ] **[R]** Contact and escalation channel with the service provider documented.

## B. Business case

- [ ] **[M]** Business case approved by the sponsor — *Evidence: T-02 with approval record.*
- [ ] **[M]** Business case contains measurable objectives (e.g., total cost of ownership, availability, recovery time, provisioning agility, mobility, continuity).
- [ ] **[M]** Budget defined, including a reserve for post-migration operating costs.
- [ ] **[R]** Alternatives considered (including "do not migrate") and documented.
- [ ] **[R]** Risk appetite stated by leadership.

## C. Privileged Access Policy

- [ ] **[M]** Privileged Access Policy approved by leadership — *Evidence: T-04 with approval record.*
- [ ] **[M]** Policy defines what privileged access is.
- [ ] **[M]** Policy defines who may grant privileged access, for how long and with what evidence.
- [ ] **[M]** Policy includes a segregation-of-duties rule suited to the team size.
- [ ] **[M]** If there is a single administrator: approval of elevations assigned to a business manager or to the service provider as a compensating control.
- [ ] **[R]** Policy covers emergency (break-glass) access and sanctions.

## D. Indicator baseline

- [ ] **[M]** Baseline recorded — *Evidence: T-03.*
- [ ] **[M]** At least **five benefit indicators**, each with formula, source and initial value.
- [ ] **[M]** At least **three exposure indicators** (e.g., number of standing administrators, privileged accounts without MFA, shared administrative accounts), each with formula, source and initial value.
- [ ] **[R]** Target value defined for each indicator.
